Home Finance — last updated October 2026

Personal use only

Home Finance is a private budgeting tool used by its owner for their own household's accounts. It is not a commercial service, and no account is offered to anyone else.

Bank access

Connecting a bank account gives the app read-only access to the accounts you select, through Enable Banking. You can withdraw that access at any time from your bank's app, and it expires automatically at the end of the consent period.

No financial advice

Totals, categories and "money left" figures are estimates produced automatically and may be wrong. They are not financial advice. Your bank's own records are always authoritative.
