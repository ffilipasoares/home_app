Privacy

Home Finance — last updated October 2026

Home Finance is a private, personal budgeting app. It is used by its owner only, for their own household's accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app reads

With your consent, given on your bank's own screen, the app reads the accounts you choose through Enable Banking, a licensed account information service provider. It reads account details, balances and transactions. This access is read-only: the app cannot make payments or change anything in your bank.

What the app stores

For each transaction: date, amount, currency, merchant or description, and which account it came from, plus the category you or the app assign to it. Data is stored in a Google Firebase project owned by the app's owner and is readable only by the owner's signed-in Google account.

What the app does not do

  • It never sees your bank login, password, PIN or approval codes.
  • It does not sell, share or publish your data.
  • It does not make payments.

How long access lasts

Bank access expires automatically at the end of the consent period. You can withdraw it at any time from your bank's app (connected apps / open banking settings), which stops any further reads immediately.

Processors

Enable Banking Oy (bank connection), Google Firebase / Google Cloud (storage, hosting and transaction categorisation).
